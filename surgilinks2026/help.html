Help & Information

Scalpel Points & Ranks

Your points are shown in the top left of the screen. You gain points by using the app every day and by answering questions successfully. You lose points by answering questions incorrectly. There are new ranks to discover every 300 points.

Questions

The questions are generated by AI. They are not necessarily guaranteed to be correct or at the correct difficulty level. Please check if you think the answers given are inaccurate.
