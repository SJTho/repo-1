Privacy Policy

 

Last updated: September 2026

 

Introduction

 

NeuroReg.app is committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal data responsibly. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the rights available to you.

 

Information We Collect

 

When you create an account, we may collect and store the following information:

 
  • Email address
  • Nickname (username)
  • Password (stored securely in encrypted or hashed form)
  • Revision scores and performance data within the application
 

How We Use Your Information

 

Your information is used solely to:

 
  • Create and manage your account.
  • Authenticate and secure access to NeuroReg.app.
  • Store and display your revision scores and progress.
  • Maintain and improve the functionality of the application.
 

Data Sharing

 

NeuroReg.app does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

 

Information may be disclosed only where required by law or to protect the security and integrity of the service.

 

Data Security

 

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are taken to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse, or disclosure.

 

Data Retention

 

Your information is retained for as long as your account remains active or as required for the operation of NeuroReg.app.

 

Your Rights

 

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.

 

Cookies

 

NeuroReg.app may use essential cookies to support site functionality, user preferences, and login sessions.

 

Changes to This Policy

 

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.

 

Contact

 

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact us via the details provided on the Contact page.
