About NeuroReg.app

Mission

Designed for trainees in Neurosurgery by a retired Consultant Neurosurgeon and senior Neurosurgical trainer, the app presents questions that are suitable for post-graduate clinicians throughout the world especially those preparing for the UK FRCS or MRCS examinations. By presenting MCQs in a gamified environment exam revision becomes effortless and even fun. Features like friends and weekly emailed questions are designed to produce a community of learners and the links to other training sites are designed to act as a training hub.

Free

Yes really, it's completely free, training is expensive enough. We might add adverts later on to generate a bit of profit but we will not be charging users.

Your data

Our approach to data is to keep as little as possible and you can delete it whenever you want. Please see the privacy policy for full details.
