Cookie Policy

What are cookies?

 

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly and remember your preferences.

 

How NeuroReg.app uses cookies

 

NeuroReg.app uses only essential cookies required for the operation of the website, including:

 
  • Maintaining secure user login sessions.
  • Remembering cookie consent preferences.
  • Supporting core website functionality.
 

Third-party cookies

 

NeuroReg.app does not use third-party advertising cookies.

 

Managing cookies

 

Most web browsers allow you to control, block, or delete cookies through browser settings. Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect the functionality of the website.

 

Changes to this policy

 

This Cookie Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page.
